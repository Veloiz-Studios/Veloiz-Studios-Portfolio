01 // THE CHALLENGE

High-ticket financial advisory faces a crisis of digital credibility.

The industry standard is riddled with outdated conventions—generic stock photos of handshakes, clunky PDF brochures, and uninspired layouts. For a firm handling millions in corporate tax optimization, their digital presence often felt like an afterthought.

Complex service offerings, such as IND-AS compliance and enterprise structuring, are notoriously difficult to communicate to busy executive decision-makers. The friction in the user journey meant that pre-qualifying leads before a consultation call was almost impossible, leading to wasted hours on mismatched inquiries.

02 // TECHNICAL EXECUTION

Precision engineering met editorial design.

A

Stacked Sticky Capabilities Engine

A custom CSS sticky card-stacking mechanic that elegantly unveils services as the user scrolls, featuring monospaced metadata and oversized watermark indices to guide the eye.

B

Interactive Corporate Tax Estimator

A real-time, client-side calculator engineered to let CFOs dynamically select entity types and turnover brackets, instantly viewing required compliance tiers without page reloads.

C

Wall Street Editorial Aesthetics

Leveraged high-contrast typography paired with stark monospaced badges (IND-AS COMPLIANT) and exactly zero stock photos for uncompromising trust.

03 // Business Impact

3.5x

Higher time-on-page via interactive widgets and sticky card mechanics.

Pre-qualified consultation leads with clear budget expectations.

Perceived agency design polish positioning the firm alongside global practices.
